# ClimateSphere v1.0.0 — First Official Release

Welcome to the official **v1.0.0** release of **ClimateSphere**, featuring both the high-performance planetary web control room and the brand-new, 100% native Android mobile application!

---

## 🤖 Native Android Application Highlights

- **⚡ Instant 0ms Cold Startup**: Room SQLite database acts as the Single Source of Truth, emitting cached weather immediately upon opening while synchronizing fresh telemetry in the background.
- **📍 Smart Auto-Locate & Dual Reverse-Geocoding**: 
  - Automatically queries Google Play Services `FusedLocationProviderClient`.
  - Native `android.location.Geocoder` resolves coordinates into your real city, state, and country names, backed by a secondary network fallback engine.
- **🎨 Material You Dynamic Themed App Icon (Android 13+)**:
  - Full adaptive icon support with `<monochrome>` layer that dynamically tints with your device's wallpaper palette.
- **🖤 AMOLED Pure Black Aesthetic**: Battery-saving `#000000` deep space black interface with glowing `#06B6D4` cyan highlights.
- **📊 Synoptic Dashboard**:
  - Hero temperature card with apparent temp, humidity, wind, and surface pressure.
  - 24-hour horizontal forecast scroll with rain percentages.
  - EPA Air Quality Index with PM2.5, PM10, Carbon Monoxide, and Ozone breakdown.
  - 7-day extended forecast with thermal range bars.
- **🔍 Global City Search**: Instant debounced search powered by Open-Meteo Geocoding.
