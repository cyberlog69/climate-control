# ClimateSphere v1.5.1 — Skeuomorphic Material 3 Theme & Tactical UI Polish

We are excited to release **v1.5.1** of **ClimateSphere**! This patch release introduces an exquisite **Skeuomorphic Material Design 3 Visual Overhaul** across both the Web platform and the native Android application (`com.climatesphere.app`), blending tactile environmental instrument ergonomics with modern Material 3 surfaces.

---

## 🎨 Key Highlights & Polish

### 1. 🎛️ Tactical Skeuomorphic Material 3 Overhaul (Web & Android)
- **Instrument Bezels & Recessed Sockets**: Multi-layered extruded chassis borders, inner shadow recesses (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`), and metallic specular top-edge rim reflections (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`).
- **Tactile Mechanical Switchgear**: Tactile push buttons and toggle selectors with physics-based mechanical depression states (`active:translate-y-0.5`), milled bevel edges, and brushed steel gradient backings.
- **Glass-Faced Dials & Analog Gauges**: Convex glare reflections and calibrated measurement ticks on atmospheric barometers, hygrometers, and wind indicators.
- **Vacuum Fluorescent & LED Display Tubes**: High-contrast illuminated phosphors with subtle glow filters for mission-critical telemetry readouts.
- **Seamless Material 3 Surface Harmonization**: Embedded Material Design 3 tonal elevations and container palettes (`surfaceContainerHighest`, `surfaceContainerLow`) for both daytime light mode and nocturnal dark mode.

### 2. 📱 Native Android Compose Ergonomics
- **Embossed Card Containers**: Upgraded `HomeScreen`, `WildfireRadarCard`, `RenewableEnergyCard`, `DualCityCard`, and `ClimateSimulatorCard` with skeuomorphic dual-tone highlights and tactile card elevations.
- **Precision Sweeping Radar & Diurnal Curves**: Refined anti-aliased radar scanning rings, pulsating thermal anomaly blips, and smooth photovoltaic/wind generation diurnal curves.
- **Target SDK 35 Readiness**: Built with Android Gradle Plugin 8.7.3 and Jetpack Compose 2024.11.00 targeting Android 15 (API 35).

### 3. 🌐 Web & OTA Updates
- **Vite Production Optimization**: 100% clean production build and asset chunking.
- **In-App OTA Release Gateway**: Updated OTA update checker in `githubReleaseApi.js` to automatically detect, verify, and offer direct APK downloads for `v1.5.1`.
